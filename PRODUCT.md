## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React/Vinext through OpenAI Sites, chosen for a responsive, motion-rich one-page experience that can be privately published and evolved into future campaign landing pages.

## Users

Primary audience inferred from the explicit brief: owners and commercial leaders of small and mid-sized businesses that have disconnected marketing, sales and operational tools and want a single partner to structure the digital operation.

## Product Purpose

Present and sell Frontier Vision's integrated digital services. The landing page must make it clear that Frontier does not sell isolated deliverables; it connects acquisition, presence, CRM, automation, artificial intelligence, systems and measurement so the business can operate and grow with less friction.

Success for this surface is a qualified visitor understanding the integrated offer quickly and starting a project conversation.

## Positioning

Frontier Vision combines strategy, design, code, automation, media and data as one operating system for growth. The distinctive mechanism is the connected flow: attention becomes demand, demand enters an organized pipeline, repetitive work is automated and decisions return through dashboards.

## Operating Context

The offer covers premium landing pages, institutional sites and e-commerce; CRM; AI agents; automations; custom systems; SEO; Google Business Profile; strategic paid media; digital structure; and dashboards. The current surface is the first of a future family of focused landing pages for e-commerce, social media and traditional agency services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single persuasive landing page in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Primary action: start a project conversation. Final production contact destination is still undecided, so the current build must use an honest, easily replaceable contact action and must not invent an email, phone number or WhatsApp number.
- Narrative must connect all services instead of showing a generic catalog.
- Mobile and desktop experiences must both preserve legibility, motion hierarchy and a clear action.
- Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
- No invented clients, testimonials, revenue figures, performance promises or case-study outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

- Parent brand: Frontier Vision.
- Product name selected for this offer: Frontier OS.
- Supplied logo must be preserved as the primary brand mark.
- Supplied logo palette: Frontier red `#9C311F` and warm ivory `#E6DBCD`.
- Requested direction: cinematic, premium and Apple-like in its restraint, pacing and product focus.
- Reference sites supplied by the user: AfterCode and Walk Productions. Use them as composition, typography, section-rhythm and motion references without copying their identity or claims.
- Avoid generic template aesthetics and obvious AI-generated styling.
- Tone: direct, strategic, confident and useful; avoid bureaucratic copy and empty hype.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the connected system, not a list of disconnected services.
2. Demonstrate the flow from attention to operation to intelligence.
3. Earn trust through clarity, craft and concrete mechanisms instead of unsupported claims.
4. Make the next step obvious without pressuring the visitor.
5. Build a reusable visual language that can extend to future service-specific landing pages.
