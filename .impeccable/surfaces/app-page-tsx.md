# Frontier OS landing page

## Scope and mode

Single-route persuasive landing page for owners and commercial leaders. The visitor must understand the integrated offer, see how the parts connect, and start a project conversation.

## Chosen direction

Frontier Operations Manual: a living industrial field manual translated into a cinematic product reveal. Approved north-star: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-c.png`; comp B contributes only the discipline of integrating the supplied motion asset as a horizontal cinematic strip. The layout must not literalize fake instrument readings, dashboard metrics or commercial claims from generated references.

## Memorable moment

The Frontier flame is the system core. A single red route leaves it and resolves into four connected outcomes: presence, acquisition, operation and intelligence.

## Component grammar

- Square or near-square corners; no decorative pill containers.
- Hairline graphite rules and registration marks; red is reserved for routed movement and action.
- Deep matte black fields alternate with committed brick-red and warm-ivory sections.
- Display type is tall and condensed; body type is neutral and highly legible.
- Elevation comes from controlled media depth, not shadowed cards.
- Motion is one connected route plus the supplied video; content remains visible without animation.

## Unresolved production decisions

The final contact destination and verified proof assets are not supplied. Calls to action must remain honest placeholders that scroll to the contact close without inventing details.
